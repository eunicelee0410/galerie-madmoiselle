Exhibition fees and payment

Set what artists pay for a short exhibition in Room 2 or the viewing room. A price left empty shows as "Ask us". Payment happens on your payment provider's own secure page, such as Stripe, Toss Payments, PayPal or KakaoPay, so paste the payment link you created there. Leave both the link and the bank details empty to hide payment from the front page.

Open calls

Each open call appears on the front page with its own deadline and entry fee. Artists send their application to the email under Website first; the payment details for the call's fee appear only after they send it. For Toss Payments, make a payment link (링크페이) for the entry fee in your Toss Payments merchant admin and paste it here. A call without its own link uses the payment link and bank details under Exhibition fees and payment.
